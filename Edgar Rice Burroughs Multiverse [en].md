# 🌌 The Complete Chronicle of the Edgar Rice Burroughs Multiverse

> **The Definitive Timeline Guide.** All books are ordered strictly by the chronological sequence of in-universe events (from ancient times to the 25th century).

---

### 🦖 1. THE ANCIENT ERA (The Primeval Past)

The prehistoric period before the main characters begin their journeys in the 20th century.

*   **🌍 Earth (circa 100,000 B.C.)**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Eternal Savage" (Main Story)** — *The heroine from the 20th century is mystically transported back to Earth's primeval past (the Niocene epoch). There she survives among dinosaurs and saber-toothed tigers alongside the caveman Nu.*
*   **🔴 Mars (Barsoom) — Millennia B.C.**
    *   *Timeline events:* The red planet's vast oceans begin to dry up. The ancient races construct a global network of canals and slowly fade away in endless wars over receding water and air resources.
*   **☁️ Venus (Amtor) — Millennia B.C.**
    *   *Timeline events:* The planet experiences the golden age of a single, unified civilization. It later collapses entirely due to a planet-wide plague that wipes out most women, shattering the global empire into isolated city-states. These historical chronicles are later revealed in **"Lost on Venus"**.

---

### 🌍 2. THE EARLY 20th CENTURY (The Age of Great Heroes & The Inner World)

The era where the heroes of Earth, Mars, and the inner world make their first discoveries within the same parallel timeframe.

*   **🤠 1866 — John Carter Arrives on Mars**
    *   The American Civil War veteran falls asleep in an Arizona cave and mystically wakes up on Barsoom, where he eventually becomes its ruler.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"A Princess of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Gods of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Warlord of Mars"**

*   **🐒 1888 — The Birth of Tarzan**
    *   In Africa, a son is born to Lord and Lady Greystoke right before their tragic deaths. He is adopted and raised by apes.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Tarzan of the Apes"** — *The origin of the hero, his childhood, and his survival in the jungle (events lead up to 1909 when Tarzan turns 20 years old).*

*   **🔴 1896–1910 — The Chronicles of Carter's Children**
    *   John Carter’s grown children (his son Carthoris and daughter Tara) enter their own wars across the dying red planet.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Thuvia, Maid of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Chessmen of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Master Mind of Mars"**

*   **⛏️ Early 1910s — The Discovery of the Hollow Earth (Pellucidar)**
    *   Earthmen use a mechanical tunneling machine, the "Iron Mole," to bore through the planet's crust. They discover an inner world inhabited by dinosaurs, existing simultaneously with the 20th-century surface.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"At the Earth's Core"** — *David Innes's first expedition.*
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Pellucidar"** — *The establishment of the Empire of Pellucidar.*

*   **🌍 1913–1914 — The Portal at Tarzan's Estate**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Eternal Savage" (Intro & Finale)** — *The story unfolds in real-time at Tarzan's African estate. An earthquake opens a cave, bringing the caveman Nu into the 20th century while the heroine slips back into the past.*
    *   *Lore Note: Mathematically, Tarzan should be 25–26 years old here. However, Burroughs describes him as a mature 35-year-old man with a teenage son, accelerating the universe's internal timeline for the sake of the plot.*

---

### 📡 3. THE 1920s: THE GREAT UNIFICATION

The turning point where separate worlds finally connect into a single unified multiverse via communication technology.

*   **📡 The Invention of Interplanetary Radio ("Gridley Waves")**
    *   Young scientist Jason Gridley invents a unique type of radio wave capable of piercing space and planet crusts. He picks up an SOS from a subterranean prison, followed immediately by the first contact from John Carter on Mars.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Tanar of Pellucidar"**
*   **🤝 Late 1920s — The Rescue Expedition**
    *   Learning that the inner world is in danger, Gridley rushes to Africa to find Tarzan. Together, they build a massive rigid airship and fly into the Hollow Earth. From this point forward, Earth, Mars, and Pellucidar remain in constant communication.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Tarzan at the Earth's Core"** — *The ultimate crossover novel.*
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Back to the Stone Age"**

---

### 🚀 4. THE 1930s–1940s: THE INTERPLANETARY ERA

Events on Mars, Venus, Jupiter, and deep space unfold simultaneously.

*   **☁️ 1931 — Launch to Venus (Amtor)**
    *   Earthman Carson Napier listens to Gridley’s radio broadcasts from Mars and builds a rocket to fly to John Carter. However, he miscalculates his trajectory and crashes into the clouds of Venus.
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Pirates of Venus"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Lost on Venus"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Carson of Venus"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Escape on Venus"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"The Wizard of Venus"**

*   **🔴 1930s–1940s: Parallel Events on Mars**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"A Fighting Man of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Swords of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Synthetic Men of Mars"**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Llana of Gathol"**

*   **✨ 1940 — The Quantum Transfer to Poloda**
    *   As World War II rages on Earth, American pilot Tyler Pope is shot down over France. At the moment of death, his soul is instantly transferred to the planet Poloda, thousands of light-years away, which is locked in its own total war.
    *   📖 *Series:* **"Beyond the Farthest Star"**:
        *   *Part 1:* **"Adventure on Poloda"**
        *   *Part 2:* **"Tangor Returns"**     

*   **⚡ Early 1940s — Abducted to Jupiter**
    *   The brutal Skeleton Men of Jupiter arrive in spaceships from the Jovian abyss and kidnap John Carter directly from Mars.
    *   📖 *Novella:* **"Skeleton Men of Jupiter"**

*   **🦖 1944 — Parallel Events in the Hollow Earth**
    *   📖 *Book:* **"Savage Pellucidar"** — *The final conclusion of the inner world story arc.*

---

### ⚔️ 5. THE 21st–25th CENTURIES: EARTH'S DARK FUTURE

Earth exchanges advanced technology with Mars, which inadvertently triggers a planetary catastrophe.

*   **🚀 2026 — The Hollow Moon Trap**
    *   An Earth spaceship launches for Mars but, due to a saboteur, crashes into the interior of the hollow Moon. The lunar barbarians, the Kalkars, steal the Earthmen's rocket technology blueprints.
    *   📖 *Part 1:* **"The Moon Maid"**

*   **⚔️ 2122 — The Lunar Invasion**
    *   The lunar monsters build a massive armada, invade Earth, destroy its cities, and completely enslave humanity for centuries.
    *   📖 *Part 2:* **"The Moon Men"**

*   **🏹 The 2400s (25th Century) — The Grand Finale**
    *   After centuries of oppression, the feral remnants of humanity gather into clans and launch a massive rebellion, permanently freeing Earth from its alien conquerors.
    *   📖 *Part 3:* **"The Red Hawk"**
